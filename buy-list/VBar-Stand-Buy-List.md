# VBar Stand - Hardware Buy List

This VBar implementation uses the retained-pivot and detent hardware proven on the iX14 and Spirit WAVE stands, with **M3 × 10 mm cap screws** for the VBar-specific clamps.

## Heat-set inserts

- [ ] **6 × M3 inserts**, approximately **5 mm outside diameter × 5 mm long** - four in the case bodies for cap screws, one in each pivot-stub stand block
- [ ] **1 × M4 insert**, approximately **6 mm outside diameter × 6 mm long** - right case body for the ball plunger

## Pivot and detent hardware

- [ ] **1 × M4 × 0.7 spring-loaded ball plunger**, approximately 8-9 mm threaded body with an approximately 2.5 mm ball (tested spring range: 3-15 N)
- [ ] **2 × M4 × 20 mm socket-head or button-head screws**
- [ ] **2 × M4 Nyloc nuts**
- [ ] **2 × M4 flat washers**

## Clamp and stand hardware

- [ ] **4 × M3 × 10 mm socket-head or button-head screws** - two for each case cap
- [ ] **2 × M3 × 8 mm button-head screws** - keyed stand/header mounts
- [ ] **2 × M3 flat washers** - stand mounting screws

## Assembly supplies

- [ ] **Thin double-sided tape**, one narrow strip on top of the handle at each clamp location
- [ ] Optional: a small amount of **removable, non-permanent threadlocker** for the ball-plunger threads

The two caps are different left/right parts. A shallow `L`/`R` on each cap and body faces inward when mounted, as seen from the front of the radio. Do not substitute the Spirit WAVE's M3 × 12 mm cap screws.
